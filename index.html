Hybrid Performance Redefined

The Steam Machine isn't just a console; it's a fully-realized high-end gaming PC in a compact, living-room-ready form factor. Powered by the Linux-based SteamOS, it provides a dedicated 10-foot interface for your TV while maintaining the open-source freedom of a desktop computer.

Switch to Desktop Mode instantly to browse the web, install productivity software, or customize your setup. Why settle for a closed ecosystem when you can have the flexibility of a workstation with the plug-and-play simplicity of hardware designed for play?

  • Access 50,000+ Steam titles natively on SteamOS
  • Runs Windows for full PC library compatibility
  • Free online multiplayer — no subscription required
  • Compact design fits any TV stand or media cabinet

Unmatched Value

Skip the "console tax." Access your existing Steam library without rebuying titles and enjoy free multiplayer gaming for life.
